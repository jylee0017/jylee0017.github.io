About me

I am a post-master's researcher in Dr. Gunsoo Kim's Auditory Processing Lab at the Korea Brain Research Institute (KBRI). I received my M.S. in Brain Science from DGIST and my B.S. in Software Engineering from Hanyang University (ERICA).

My research examines how neural representations of vocalizations change across acoustic environments. Building on this work, I aim to uncover the neural computations that transform sensory information into action, particularly how neural population dynamics support action selection and modulate ongoing movements.

Research interests: Sensory Coding, Sensorimotor Integration, Computational Neuroscience, and Brain–Computer Interfaces.

Fellowship: Junior Researcher Fellowship, KBRI (2025–2028).
