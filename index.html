Auditory neuroscience · Neural coding · Behavior

Understanding how the brain represents sound.

I am a post-master's researcher in Dr. Gunsoo Kim's Auditory Processing Lab at the Korea Brain Research Institute (KBRI). My work investigates how neural representations of vocalizations change across different acoustic environments.

I am interested in how neural populations encode behaviorally relevant sensory information and how these representations contribute to perception, decisions, and actions.

Research interests: Neural population coding · Auditory and sensory processing · Sensorimotor integration · Computational neuroscience

Neural Coding of Vocalizations in Background Noise
